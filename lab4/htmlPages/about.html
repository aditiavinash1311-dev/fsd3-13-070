Our Evolution

How AURA Was Born.

From a garage optical prototype to an international tech ecosystem.

2021 • FOUNDING

The Spark of Innovation

Founded by former aerospace hardware engineers in Silicon Valley with a mission to eliminate bloatware and build unyielding, high-craft mobile devices.

"We asked ourselves: Why should phones compromise between camera power and battery longevity?"
Over 1,200 prototype iterations before achieving the ultra-thin Titanium chassis architecture.
2023 • BREAKTHROUGH

First Quantum Bionic Chipset

Patented our custom 3nm micro-architecture neural engine, drastically reducing heat while enabling real-time computational photography on-device.

2025 • SCALING

Global Launch & Zero-Carbon Pledge

Expanded distribution to 120+ countries and transitioned all manufacturing plants to 100% renewable solar power with modular repairability.

Awarded 2025 Green Tech Pioneer for 100% plastic-free packaging and recycled titanium frames.
